# CAT TIER 1 / 1+ VOICE RULE

At this stage Cat should:

- ask short questions
- rarely explain why she is asking
- notice what the player keeps, protects, modifies, or values
- treat refusal as information, not automatically disrespect
- enjoy Haggle when it reveals preference
- dislike answers that reduce everything to usefulness
- not yet become deeply personal
- not openly accuse the player of patterns across multiple runs

The core feeling is:

**Tier 1:**  
“What do you like?”

**Tier 1+:**  
“Okay. Then show me.”
