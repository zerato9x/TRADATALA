# TRADATALA 1.1 — The Zodiac Boss System Update

The Zodiac visitors have arrived at the sidewalk table.

Version 1.1 brings **eleven playable Zodiac bosses** to TRADATALA: Rooster, Cat, Dog, Monkey, Pig, Buffalo, Horse, Goat, Rat, Tiger, and Snake. Each changes how you approach the evening Deal, from Rooster's closing register and Cat's locked cards to Snake's commands.

Your encounters begin before the boss battle. Meet the day's visitor at Noon, choose your answers, and decide which promises you can keep. The Afternoon return judges what you actually did at the table. Those encounters shape the evening challenge and build relationships that remember your history across runs.

This update also adds:

- Authored English and Vietnamese conversations, counteroffers, and character-specific commitments.
- Relationship progression through Kindred, Last Chance encounters, and private Emblem scenes.
- Three boss difficulty tiers, permanent Zodiac history, and collectible Emblems.
- Three separate save profiles, each with its own run and progression.
- A clearer table, animated boss effects, improved collections and receipts, and expanded Handbook entries.
- Updated music direction and soundtrack cues for the campaign.

The seven-day campaign and Endless continuation are available in the browser and Windows builds. Dragon's encounter remains reserved for a future update.

Thanks for playing. See you at the table!
