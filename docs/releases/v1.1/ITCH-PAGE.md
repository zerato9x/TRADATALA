Tagline: A solo Vietnamese Rummy roguelike: build your deck, meet the Zodiac, survive the debt.

## Browser: 1.0.3 | Windows download: 1.1

**Update - 15 October 2026:** Browser play has been rolled back to version 1.0.3 while bugs in 1.1 are fixed and further revisions are prepared. Version 1.1 remains available as a Windows download. The features and screenshots below describe the Windows 1.1 update.

TRADATALA is a solo Phỏm roguelike set around a Vietnamese sidewalk table. Build Sets and Runs, extend your Melds, use Drinks to change your options, and earn enough VND to survive seven days of escalating debt.

## Windows 1.1 — The Zodiac Boss System Update

A major overhaul of the game: eleven Zodiac bosses, deeper deckbuilding, rebuilt Fortune and card transformation systems, an animated helper, three save files, and a refreshed table and interface.

## Meet the day's Zodiac

Talk to the day's visitor at Noon, negotiate promises, face their Afternoon judgment, and adapt to their rules in the Evening Deal. Authored English and Vietnamese encounters build relationships across runs, through Kindred, Last Chance encounters, and collectible Emblems.

Rooster, Cat, Dog, Monkey, Pig, Buffalo, Horse, Goat, Rat, Tiger, and Snake are playable, each with three boss difficulty tiers. Dragon is reserved for a future update.

## Build your deck at the street table

Buy Relics and playing cards from Hàng Rong, or pay to remove a card from the current run. Every owned Relic is active. Visit Gieo Quẻ for Fortune, Gold, Black Ink, Liquid, Negative, and Glitch card treatments. From Tuesday onward, Đánh Giày can transform the Rank or Suit of a committed pair of cards. Your choices reshape the deck for later Deals.

## Twelve Drinks, more ways to play

Choose a Drink for its card-flow ability, then use the table's target and charge cues to act. All twelve have new full, half, and empty artwork. Sâm dứa and Bạc xỉu let you mark specific cards to carry from Phase 1 into Phase 2.

## Learn at your own pace

Ask Strawy for contextual help, a pointing tour, or a preview of a suggested move. Use the searchable English/Vietnamese Handbook for full rules. Tutorial, First Seed, and Strawy visibility are independent settings.

## Seven days, then Endless

Balance spending on your deck against the money needed for the nightly collector. Finish the seven-day campaign and continue into Endless. Three named save files keep separate runs, unlocks, Zodiac histories, and Emblems.

## And then there’s the music.

12 Zodiac animals. 12 different subgenres of funk. 6 regional and traditional Vietnamese musical identities.

Each Zodiac theme takes a different corner of Vietnamese music and throws it headfirst into funk, from deep funk and soul to harder, stranger grooves built around sounds from across Vietnam.

Twelve animals. Twelve flavors of funk. One very Vietnamese card game.

Choose the CAT or DOG Authored DJ set, or browse the 26-track playlist with shuffle and repeat. The soundtrack spans twelve Zodiac identities; version 1.1 currently has eleven playable bosses.
