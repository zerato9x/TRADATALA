# TRADATALA 1.0.2 — prepared itch.io description

TRADATALA is a solo Phỏm roguelike set around a Vietnamese sidewalk table. Build Sets and Runs, use Drinks to twist the rules, and survive seven days of escalating debts in VND.

Play four Deals each day, with visits to Cô Trà Đá, the fortune teller, shoe-shine service, lottery seller, and relic vendor between Deals. Modify your persistent deck, collect and equip relics, unlock Drinks, and decide how to spend your earnings before the next collection.

Pay Sunday's 16 million VND debt to complete the week. Review your cards, finances, and run history, then start again or continue into Endless. Seeded runs and autosaves let you return to the table.

The soundtrack brings twelve Zodiac themes, Vietnamese musical identities, and different flavors of funk together. The reactive Cat/Dog DJ routes alternate across campaign days; the full 26-track jukebox is also available.

Play in your desktop browser or download the Windows version. Vietnamese and English are available in Options. Use How to Play or Practice from the title menu to learn the table.

This release contains the complete current seven-day campaign. Zodiac bosses and daily Zodiac modifiers, multiplayer, and AI opponents are not implemented.
