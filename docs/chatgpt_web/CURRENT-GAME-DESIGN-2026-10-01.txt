SHIPPED V1.0.3 BASELINE
TRADATALA is a single-player Phỏm roguelike set at a Vietnamese sidewalk table.
- A Deal has two Phases with four mandatory discards and a player-confirmed LAST CALL window in each Phase. Melds persist between Phases. Sets use one rank; Runs use one suit and consecutive ranks, Ace low with no wrap.
- Scoring, MÓM, Ù, Ù Khan, deadwood, exhaustion, and settlement follow the documented shared rules. See the in-game Handbook for player-facing rules.
- The campaign has seven days and four Deals per day. Monday–Sunday debt targets are 250,000; 500,000; 1,000,000; 2,000,000; 4,000,000; 8,000,000; and 16,000,000 VNĐ. The collector takes the target once after the evening Deal. Paying Sunday completes the campaign; Endless starts at 24,000,000 VNĐ and raises the target by 50% per day.
- A new run starts with VNĐ25,000. Wallet payments and payouts commit through the VndWallet journal. Six current NPC roles are collector, Cô Trà Đá, fortune teller / Gieo Quẻ, shoe shine, lottery seller, and Hàng Rong relic vendor.
- All twelve Drinks have implemented mechanics. Gieo Quẻ permanently transforms physical campaign cards. Seeded runs, independent gameplay RNG streams, autosave/recovery, a relic collection with up to four equipped relics, a Sunday report, and Endless are part of the released baseline.
- The v1.0.3 tag includes the fixes and pricing described in the release record. Use the tag/build manifest for exact release contents.
