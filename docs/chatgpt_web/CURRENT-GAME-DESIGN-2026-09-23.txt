SHIPPED GAMEPLAY BASELINE
TRADATALA is a single-player Phỏm roguelike at a Vietnamese sidewalk table. The release combines card play, a seven-day debt campaign, persistent deck changes, NPC services, Drinks, relics, seeded runs and an Endless continuation.

Core Deal:
- A Deal has two Phases. Each Phase has four mandatory discards and a player-confirmed LAST CALL window.
- Melds persist between Phases. A Set uses cards of one rank; a Run uses one suit and consecutive ranks, with Ace low and no wrap.
- Scoring resolves new Melds, extensions, Deadwood and settlement through the shared scoring pipeline. MÓM is evaluated per Phase. Ù is Phase-scoped; Ù Khan uses the documented near-meld rule. Exhaustion triggers only on a requested draw/refill from an already-empty draw deck and resumes the request after recycling.
- The in-game Handbook is bilingual and searchable. Monday onboarding uses real cards and optional contextual hints; it does not grant fake outcomes or replace the rules.

Campaign and economy:
- Seven days, four Deals per day, and Starter/Morning/Noon/Afternoon event slots. Weekday targets: Monday 250,000; Tuesday 500,000; Wednesday 1,000,000; Thursday 2,000,000; Friday 4,000,000; Saturday 8,000,000; Sunday 16,000,000 VNĐ.
- The collector takes the daily target once after the evening Deal. A shortfall ends the run; paying Sunday completes the campaign. Endless starts at 24,000,000 VNĐ and grows the daily target by 50%, preserving the run.
- New runs begin with VNĐ25,000. Wallet, score conversion and payment remain integer VND authorities. Service choices and payouts commit through the wallet journal.
- The full campaign has six NPC roles: collector, Cô Trà Đá, fortune teller / Gieo Quẻ, shoe shine, lottery seller and Hàng Rong relic vendor. Events own their participant lists and service windows.

Build systems:
- All twelve Drinks have implemented mechanics. Availability follows day gates and persistent unlock goals; Drink effects have their own timing and targeting rules.
- Gieo Quẻ makes permanent transformations to physical cards. Relics are offered from seeded per-visit inventories; a player can own a collection and equip up to four.
- Runs can use an entered or generated seed. Gameplay randomness uses versioned independent streams. Autosaves capture committed gameplay and exact draw/RNG continuation; backup recovery is available. Drink unlock progress is separate from the active run.
- A Sunday report records run statistics and can continue into Endless or start again.
- The released v1.0.3 tag includes fixes for Tuesday progression, collector audio lifecycle, NPC interaction, Handbook input blocking, money HUD hierarchy, major payout cues and SET-extension scoring. It also adds debt-based repeat pricing for polish, tips and lottery tickets. See the release record for exact price formulas and acceptance evidence.
