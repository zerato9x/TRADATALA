CURRENT WORKSPACE MUSIC BEHAVIOR — 2026-09-29 WIP
- The Escape/Menu jukebox offers Authored DJ and Playlist through one mode selector and one source chooser. DJ offers the CAT/Mèo and DOG/Chó sets; Playlist offers all 26 tracks plus Shuffle and Repeat Off/All/One.
- Starting DJ or changing its set during a run begins the approved cue for the current event/deal/phase immediately, including committed first-meld cleanup. The selected set also applies at later day openings. Switching mode or set preserves pause and does not change gameplay, wallet, history or gameplay RNG.
- Continue restores the actual recorded source, position, cue/transition, pause state, Playlist transport and active crossfade. A New Run selection does not replace the saved music on Continue. Legacy saves without a usable music checkpoint still restore gameplay and fall back to Playlist with a notice.
- Version-2 saves can optionally include value-only music checkpoints for the director, reactive controller, anti-fatigue filter and gameplay conductor. Exact music resume is available only for saves made after this checkpoint was added.
- This source work was checkpointed to `main` on September 30 and remains WIP relative to the public release. The September 29 validation gate and its limitations are recorded in `docs/CODEBASE_AUDIT_2026-09-29.md`; no new audio release/export or human listening sign-off is claimed.

SHIPPED V1.0.3 AUDIO BASELINE
- New runs use the Authored DJ route by default: the Mèo/CAT route plays on day one and the Chó/DOG route on day two; those routes alternate by day.
- The Playlist exposes all 26 OST tracks, cover art, track selection, progress, next-track preview, play/pause, shuffle and Repeat Off/All/One.
- Four frequency bands drive presentation spectrum motion.
- Gameplay cues use authored approved cue plans. MusicDirector supports holding a bar-aligned cue, releasing into original playback or the next reachable approved cue, finishing a loop before a reprise, intentional hard jumps, and release to the original track ending.
- Source WAVs remain intact. Cue control duplicates imported AudioStreamWAV data rather than editing source assets or manufacturing transition audio.
- Card choose/place/draw/shuffle cues, Drink opportunity cues, collector arrival sound and Ù / Ù Khan / Exhaustion ceremonies route through existing audio buses and event lifecycle.

AUTHORING AND EVIDENCE
- The offline MusicLoopAudition tester supports listening to candidate loops and two-bar lead-ins, reviewing approved cue sequences, and arranging bar-aligned segments for audition. Machine scores only order the listening queue; a cue is approved through human listening review.
- Timing and exhaustive loop catalogs are generated measurements. Loop seams still require listening QA.
- v1.0.3 package checks and browser startup checks do not establish final speaker/headphone mix acceptance, human listening acceptance across the full campaign, or physical-device audio behavior.
- Audio for future Zodiac boss/modifier mechanics has not been designed or approved. Do not imply that each boss already has a unique theme or reactive cue.

FILES AND OWNERS
- assets/audio/ost/ keeps the original music, measured timing, cue approvals and independent arrangement data.
- scripts/audio/gameplay_music_conductor.gd routes authored gameplay cue roles.
- audio_system/MusicDirector.gd owns audition and DJ transport.
- audio_system/MusicCueCatalog.gd owns candidate/review data.
- audio_system/MusicArrangementCatalog.gd and MusicArrangementPlayer.gd own saved audition arrangements.
- debug/MusicLoopAudition.tscn is an offline tool, not gameplay input.

ACCEPTANCE BOUNDARY
Automated routing, transport and rendered checks prove code paths under their test conditions. They do not prove that a full soundtrack mix sounds correct to a human listener. Premium audio changes need listening sign-off in addition to code, export and runtime checks.
