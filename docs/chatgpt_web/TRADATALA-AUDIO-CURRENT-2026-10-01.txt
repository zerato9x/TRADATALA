AUTHORING, OWNERSHIP, AND EVIDENCE
- assets/audio/ost/ contains original music, measured timing, cue approvals, and arrangement data.
- scripts/audio/gameplay_music_conductor.gd routes gameplay cues. audio_system/MusicDirector.gd owns DJ transport; MusicCueCatalog.gd owns cue review data; MusicArrangementCatalog.gd and MusicArrangementPlayer.gd own saved audition arrangements.
- debug/MusicLoopAudition.tscn is an offline audition tool. Machine measurements may order listening work but do not approve a cue.
- Automated routing, transport, and rendered checks prove code paths under their test conditions. They do not establish a correct full-campaign speaker/headphone mix, physical-device behavior, or human listening acceptance. Premium audio changes need listening review as well as code, export, and runtime acceptance.
